About Me

Hello! My name is Oleg. I’m a 32-year-old ABAP developer with over 10 years of professional experience. I also have solid knowledge of Oracle Siebel CRM, C#, and web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript). Throughout my career, I have had the opportunity to work on a wide range of projects, from implementing new features in complex ERP systems to optimizing existing code for better performance and maintainability. My work has often involved analyzing business requirements, designing technical solutions, and ensuring seamless integration between different software components.

I hold a degree in Business Informatics from the Faculty of Applied Mathematics, Computer Science, and Mechanics at Voronezh State University, which I completed in 2015. During my studies, I focused on programming, database management, and system analysis, which provided me with a solid foundation for my professional career. I also gained insights into business processes, which helps me communicate effectively with stakeholders and translate business needs into technical specifications. My academic background allowed me to develop strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills that I still rely on every day.

Over the years, I have accumulated extensive experience in ABAP development, including designing custom reports, interfaces, forms, and enhancements. I have worked with both SAP ECC and SAP S/4HANA environments, contributing to projects that improved system efficiency and user satisfaction. In addition, I have solid experience with Oracle Siebel CRM, where I supported and developed modules to enhance customer relationship management for large organizations. This dual expertise in ERP and CRM systems gives me a comprehensive understanding of enterprise software ecosystems and how different solutions interact with one another.

Beyond core technical skills, I have experience with web development technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which allows me to develop user-friendly interfaces and improve front-end functionality. I have also worked with C# in various internal tools and automation projects, which strengthened my programming versatility and understanding of object-oriented principles. I enjoy exploring new frameworks and libraries, as I believe continuous learning is crucial in the fast-evolving field of software development.

In summary, I am a dedicated and experienced ABAP developer with a strong technical background, a solid understanding of business processes, and a continuous drive for learning and improvement. My combination of ERP and CRM experience, web development skills, and collaborative mindset enables me to contribute effectively to complex projects and deliver high-quality solutions. I am motivated by challenging tasks, enjoy solving problems, and aim to create software that adds real value to businesses and end users.
